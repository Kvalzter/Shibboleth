A shibboleth is a word, phrase or pronunciation that tells one group of people from another, revealing who belongs and who does not. By extension, it is any custom or turn of phrase that marks someone as an insider or an outsider.

In the Book of Judges (12:5–6), the Gileadites asked fleeing Ephraimites to say the word. Their dialect had no “sh” sound, so they said sibboleth and were exposed. In the Second World War, the Dutch town name Scheveningen is said to have been used to tell native speakers from Germans, whose pronunciation gave them away.
